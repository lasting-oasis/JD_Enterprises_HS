Seasonal

Get ready before the first cold night.

A fall check catches the problems that show up the first time the heat runs: a weak igniter, a dirty flame sensor or a clogged filter.

Request a visit →

What the check covers

  • Igniter, flame sensor and burners.
  • Heat exchanger and venting.
  • Thermostat and backup heat settings.
  • Filter, blower and electrical connections.

Before the visit, you can

  • Replace the air filter.
  • Test the thermostat in heat mode on a mild day.
  • Clear storage away from the furnace or air handler.
  • Test the smoke and carbon monoxide alarms.
