Maintenance plan

A plan keeps tune-ups on the calendar.

A maintenance plan bundles the spring and fall visits your system needs, so they happen on time instead of when something breaks.

Seasonal plan

Maintenance plan details are being confirmed with J&D. Ask about seasonal tune-ups when you request a visit.

What plans usually include

  • A spring tune-up for cooling and a fall tune-up for heating.
  • A reminder when a visit is due.
  • A written summary of what the technician found.

Questions to ask about any plan

  • How many visits a year, and what each one covers.
  • Whether the plan transfers if you sell the house.
  • How to cancel, and whether it renews automatically.
