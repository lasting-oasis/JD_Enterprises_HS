Services

Heating and cooling for small businesses.

Small offices, shops and restaurants usually run packaged rooftop units or split systems. A breakdown costs business hours, so regular maintenance matters even more than at home.

Signs it's time to call

  • Hot and cold spots across the space.
  • Rising utility bills for the same hours.
  • Water stains near ceiling vents or the unit.
  • A unit that hasn't been serviced in more than a year.

What a visit covers

  • Rooftop unit inspection: belts, coils, drains and electrical.
  • Economizer and controls check, where there is one.
  • Filter changes on a set schedule.
  • A written report after each visit.

Good to know

  • Many businesses schedule maintenance every quarter, with a filter change at each visit.
  • Kitchens and salons clog filters faster than offices, so they may need more frequent changes.
