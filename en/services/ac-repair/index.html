Air conditioning that keeps up with August.

Most AC problems start small: a weak capacitor, a clogged drain or low airflow. Catching them early keeps a repair from turning into a replacement.

Signs it's time to call

  • Warm or weak air from the vents.
  • Ice on the copper lines or the indoor coil.
  • Water around the indoor unit or a tripped float switch.
  • Buzzing, clicking or a breaker that keeps tripping.

What a visit covers

  • Thermostat signal and settings.
  • Capacitor, contactor and compressor start-up.
  • Refrigerant charge and temperature split.
  • Condensate drain and coil cleanliness.

Good to know

  • A system older than about 12 to 15 years with a major failure is often worth replacing, but age alone isn't a reason to replace.
  • A system sized from a load calculation runs longer, quieter cycles and controls humidity better than an oversized one.
