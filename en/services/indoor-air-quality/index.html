Services

Cleaner air, at the right humidity.

Your HVAC system moves all the air in the house several times an hour, so it's the natural place to filter that air and control humidity.

Signs it's time to call

  • Dust that comes back days after cleaning.
  • Allergy symptoms that are worse indoors.
  • Sticky air in summer, or dry skin and static in winter.
  • Musty smells when the system runs.

What a visit covers

  • Filter type and fit, including media filter cabinets.
  • Humidity readings in different rooms.
  • Return and duct condition near the system.
  • Options sized to your system and home.

Good to know

  • Indoor humidity between about 30% and 50% is a common comfort target.
  • A higher-MERV filter only helps if the system has enough return airflow for it.
