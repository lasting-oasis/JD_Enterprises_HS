Services

Everything that moves air through your home.

Start with the service closest to what's going on. Each page explains the usual signs, what a visit covers and what to know before you decide.

How pricing works.

  • 01

    Heat pumps

    One system that cools in summer and heats in winter. Repairs, replacements and new installations.

  • 02

    AC repair and replacement

    Warm air, ice on the lines, odd noises or a unit that won't start. Diagnosis first, then a clear choice between repair and replacement.

  • 03

    Furnaces and dual fuel

    Igniters, flame sensors, heat exchangers and blowers, plus dual-fuel systems that pair a furnace with a heat pump.

  • 04

    Maintenance and tune-ups

    A spring check for cooling and a fall check for heating, with a written note of what was found.

  • 05

    Indoor air quality

    Better filters, UV lights, air purifiers and humidity control for allergies, dry winters and sticky summers.

  • 06

    Ductwork

    Sealing, inspection, cleaning and balancing for rooms that are always too hot or too cold.

  • 07

    Ductless mini-splits

    Heating and cooling for rooms the ducts don't reach: additions, garages, bonus rooms and older homes.

  • 08

    Smart thermostats

    Installation, wiring and setup, including the settings a heat pump needs so it doesn't lean on costly backup heat.

  • 09

    Light commercial

    Rooftop units and split systems for small offices, shops and restaurants, with maintenance on a set schedule.
