Accessibility statement

Last updated: November 9, 2026

J&D Enterprise Home Solutions wants everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard or zoom.

The goal

The site aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What's in place

Text meets AA contrast. Every page works with a keyboard and shows a visible focus ring. Animation stops when your device asks for reduced motion. Every page is available in English and Spanish.

Known limits

The animated system diagram on the home page is visual. The steps beside it describe the same information in text.

Report a problem

If anything on this site is hard to use, tell us on the contact page so it can be fixed.
