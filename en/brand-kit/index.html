Brand kit

How J&D looks and sounds.

Use these rules on the website, trucks, print and social posts so every place a customer meets the company feels the same.

Logo

The mark is a single loop of moving air. Use it with the name, or on its own where space is tight, like app icons and social profile pictures.

Working mark from the current website. If J&D already has a logo, it replaces this one.

Keep empty space around the logo of at least half the mark's height. Smallest size: 20 px tall on screens, 0.4 inch in print.

Download

  • Mark (SVG)
  • Logo for dark backgrounds (SVG)
  • Logo for light backgrounds (SVG)

Service icons

One line icon for each service, drawn for this site. They take the color of the text around them, so they work on dark and light backgrounds. Use them on the website, flyers, invoices and social posts.

  • Heat pumps
  • AC repair and replacement
  • Furnaces and dual fuel
  • Maintenance and tune-ups
  • Indoor air quality
  • Ductwork
  • Ductless mini-splits
  • Smart thermostats
  • Light commercial

Each name downloads that icon as an SVG file in the dark ink, for light backgrounds. To use it on a dark background, change its color attribute to the light ink.

Colors

Each color is set once in the brand file and used everywhere. Contrast is measured against the two background colors: AA means comfortable to read as normal text (4.5:1 or higher).

Type

Archivo for headlines and body text. IBM Plex Mono for small labels, numbers and data.

Voice

  • Plain and specific: say what we check, what it costs and what happens next.
  • Calm, never alarmist: no scare tactics about breakdowns.
  • Honest: no claim we can't back up, and no made-up reviews or numbers.

Do and don't

Do

  • Use the colors and fonts exactly as listed here.
  • Put the logo on Night or Paper backgrounds.
  • Write in English and Spanish with the same care.

Don't

  • Don't stretch, rotate or recolor the logo.
  • Don't set text in the Cool air or Warm air colors on Paper; they're too light to read.
  • Don't add badges, ratings or awards that haven't been earned and confirmed.
