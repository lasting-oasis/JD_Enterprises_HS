How often should I change my air filter?

Check a standard 1-inch filter every month and change it every one to three months, more often with pets, allergies or heavy use. Thicker media filters last longer; follow the label.

How often does my system need a tune-up?

At least once a year. A heat pump heats and cools, so it runs all year, and many owners schedule a spring visit and a fall visit.

Should I get a heat pump or a furnace?

In mild-winter climates a heat pump can heat efficiently most of the year. A dual-fuel system adds a gas furnace for the coldest days. The right choice depends on your home, your gas service and your budget.

What refrigerant will a new system use?

New central air conditioners and heat pumps use lower-impact refrigerants such as R-454B or R-32. Existing R-410A systems can still be serviced.

Is there a federal tax credit for a new heat pump?

The federal 25C credit ended for equipment installed after December 31, 2025. Some utility and state programs still offer rebates, so check current eligibility. For tax questions, ask a tax professional.

Why is my AC freezing up?

Usually low airflow, from a dirty filter or a blocked return, or low refrigerant. Switch the thermostat to fan only so it can thaw, replace the filter, and request a visit if it happens again.

What size system do I need?

Size comes from a load calculation, called Manual J, that looks at your home's insulation, windows, orientation and layout, not just its square footage.

What indoor humidity is best?

Between about 30% and 50%. Higher feels sticky and invites mold; lower dries out skin and wood.

Can I get service in Spanish?

This website is fully bilingual. Details on Spanish-language phone service are being confirmed.

What should I do if a carbon monoxide alarm goes off?

Get everyone outside, then call 911 or your gas company. Don't go back in until responders say it's safe.
