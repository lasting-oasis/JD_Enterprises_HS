How your system works

One loop of air.
One loop of refrigerant.(08)

Every central system is two circuits working together. Scroll through the eight parts our technicians check on every visit, and switch modes to see how a heat pump runs the same loop in reverse.

01 · Control

The thermostat makes the call.

When the room drifts past its setpoint, the thermostat sends a 24-volt signal to the indoor blower and the outdoor unit at the same time. A badly placed or miscalibrated thermostat is one of the most common causes of short-cycling and uneven rooms.

We serviceSmart thermostat installs, wiring, calibration and zoning

02 · Return air

Room air comes back through the filter.

Return grilles pull air from your living space into the system. The filter catches dust, pollen and pet dander before it reaches the coil. A clogged filter starves the system of air, raises your bill and can freeze the coil solid.

We serviceMERV 8 to 13 filters, media cabinets, return sizing

03 · Air movement

The blower keeps the air moving.

The blower wheel pushes roughly 400 cubic feet of air per minute for every ton of capacity. Weak capacitors and dirty wheels quietly cut that airflow, so the house never quite reaches temperature.

We serviceBlower motors, capacitors, ECM upgrades, wheel cleaning

04 · Indoor coil

The coil pulls heat and moisture out.

Cold refrigerant inside the evaporator coil absorbs heat from the air passing over it. Air leaves about 18 to 20 degrees cooler, and the moisture it drops runs out through the condensate drain.

We serviceCoil cleaning, drain line clearing, leak detection

05 · Suction line & compressor

The compressor does the heavy lifting.

Warm refrigerant vapor travels outside through the insulated suction line. The compressor squeezes it into a hot, high-pressure gas. It is the heart of the system and the most expensive part to replace, so we protect it on every visit.

We serviceCompressor diagnostics, hard-start kits, superheat checks

06 · Outdoor coil & fan

Heat from your house is released outside.

The condenser fan pulls outdoor air across the coil, carrying the heat away. As the refrigerant cools it condenses back into a liquid. In heating mode a heat pump flips this around and gathers heat from outdoor air instead.

We serviceCoil washing, fan motors, contactors, heat pump defrost

07 · Metering

The expansion valve resets the loop.

Liquid refrigerant returns through the small liquid line to the expansion valve. A sudden drop in pressure makes it intensely cold again, ready to absorb more heat at the coil. The refrigerant charge has to be right for this to work.

We serviceRefrigerant charge, subcooling tests, TXV replacement

08 · Delivery

Conditioned air reaches every room.

Supply ducts carry the cooled or heated air to vents throughout the house. The average duct system loses a large share of its air through leaks and loose joints, often into the attic.

We serviceDuct sealing, airflow balancing, new duct design
